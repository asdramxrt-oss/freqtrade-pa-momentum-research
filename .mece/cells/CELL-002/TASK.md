# CELL-002 — P3 Experiment Audit

## Goal
Audit the existing P3 experiment artifacts and determine what has actually
been demonstrated by repository evidence.

Inspect:
- research/experiment_results/
- relevant research documentation
- research_lib/
- user_data/configs/
- user_data/scripts/

Pay particular attention to P3-EXP-001, P3-EXP-002, and P3-EXP-003.

Do not modify experiment results.
Do not rerun expensive experiments unless necessary for verification.

Separate:
- measured results
- methodology
- interpretation
- unresolved questions

Check for:
- lookahead risk
- train/test contamination
- cost assumptions
- sample-size limitations
- robustness limitations
- reproducibility issues

When finished create:
.mece/cells/CELL-002/REPORT.md

Include exact files inspected and verification performed.
