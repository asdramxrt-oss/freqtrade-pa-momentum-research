## 1. Objective

Does the exact frozen EXP-003 long-only pullback-continuation strategy generalise
to genuinely unseen data without modification?

## 2. Hypothesis

The long-only pullback edge seen in EXP-003 (development-sample PF 1.567; 2025
OOS +14.01%) is a property of the setup and remains net-positive on a fresh,
never-evaluated period. **Falsified by a net-negative result or PF < 1.00.**

## 3. Frozen strategy version

Byte-identical to the EXP-003 implementation commit
`c4bee8e264a111aa2e72dbce203bd49db1484d8b`. `git diff c4bee8e HEAD` over
`user_data/strategies/pullback` and `user_data/strategies/shared` is empty. No
logic, parameter, stop, sizing, swing, publication-delay, depth, impulse,
confirmation or Donchian-exit change was made.

Frozen logic: confirmed swing low → later confirmed swing high, impulse ≥ 3 ATR;
close retraces 0.5–2.0 ATR within 10 bars of the impulse-high publication;
origin swing low holds; confirmation = `close > prior 3-bar high` **and**
`close > open`; entry next bar open; fixed 2 × ATR(20) stop; 1% risk sizing;
Donchian-10 exit; pivot publication delay 2 bars.

## 13. Statistical analysis

- Test: freqtrade `p_value` = **two-sided one-sample t-test**
  (`scipy.stats.ttest_1samp`) of per-trade returns (`profit_abs /
  starting_balance`) against a mean of 0.
- Sample: 64 trades. p = 0.1643 → the negative per-trade expectancy is **not**
  statistically distinguishable from zero at the 5% level.
- **Interpretation:** the fresh window provides **no evidence of a positive
  edge**, and it is under-powered (64 < 200 trades). The negative point estimate
  is not itself statistically significant. The pre-registered rule nevertheless
  classifies an absolute net-negative / PF < 1.00 outcome as FAIL, because A4 and
  A5 are absolute thresholds, not significance tests.
- No multiple-comparison adjustment (single pre-registered test).

## 16. Limitations

1. **Short window.** ~8.5 months / 64 trades is below the frozen 200-trade bar;
   inference is under-powered. A longer window does not currently exist for this
   universe (data ends 2026-09-16).
2. **The negative result is not statistically significant** (p = 0.164); the
   honest statement is "no evidence of a positive edge on fresh data", not
   "a proven losing edge".
3. **Regime.** 2025 (previous OOS) and the 2026 window were both falling markets
   (universe −34.34% and −27.48%). The strategy was less bad than buy & hold in
   both (+14.01% in 2025; −12.75% in 2026), but that is defensive behaviour, not
   a validated edge.
4. **Futures-mode** with funding excluded, matching the EXP-003 long variant.
5. **Single OOS evaluation**; it must not be re-used for selection.

## 17. Final decision

**FAILS FRESH OOS.** The EXP-003 long-only pullback result **did not replicate**
on a fresh, previously unevaluated window. The frozen strategy produced a
negative return and a profit factor below 1.00 with no statistically detectable
positive expectancy.

No tuning, no pair removal, no parameter change, no ML, no combination and no
extension/alteration of the OOS period was performed. The result is preserved as
found. Recorded in `research/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md` as **DEC-006**.

Consequence: the long-only pullback hypothesis is **not** validated. `Turtle +
Long Pullback` (the charter's original EXP-004) is **not** authorised, and
EXP-005 is not started.
