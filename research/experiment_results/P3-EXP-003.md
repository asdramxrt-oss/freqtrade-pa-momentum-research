## 1. Objective

Does funding/basis carry provide a robust **standalone** return source on the
10-perp Binance universe, net of costs — and is the return genuinely *funding*
income rather than directional exposure?

## 2. Hypothesis / null

- H: persistent funding (longs paying shorts) is harvestable net of costs, giving
  an exposure distinct from trend.
- Null: carry returns net of costs are indistinguishable from zero, or are fully
  explained by directional beta.

## 3. Method (frozen specification §6.1)

- Signal: sign of the **trailing 3-day mean funding rate** per pair.
- Position: the funding-**receiving** side (mean funding > 0 → short; < 0 → long).
- Rebalance: daily (00:00 UTC bar); flip on sign change.
- Sizing: equal notional = 10% of equity per active pair (10 positions = 1× gross).
- No threshold on |funding|; no directional stop (exit = sign flip).

Funding data was applied by the engine after fixing SI-4 (1h mark). The runner
records total funding PnL as proof.

## 5. Findings

1. **The strategy did NOT earn funding — it paid funding.** Aggregate funding PnL
   is **−11,306 USDT** over the full sample (negative = paid). A funding-harvest
   that nets to paying funding is not harvesting carry; its positive full-sample
   PF comes from **directional price exposure**, not from carry.
2. **Out-of-sample failure.** Test PF 0.805, −20.7%, p=0.405.
3. **Not statistically significant** even on the full sample (p=0.160), and the
   validation window's apparent strength (PF 2.41) is on only 100 trades.
4. **Concentration/regime risk.** The full-sample PF 1.35 is dominated by the
   positive-PF windows; the most recent window is negative.

**Direct answer to the critical question "Is carry independently useful?"** — as
preregistered, **no**: it did not deliver funding income, it failed OOS, and its
apparent edge is directional. (This is a result about *this* implementation, not
about carry in general.)

## 7. Limitations

- Single-leg perp only; no spot hedge (a true cash-and-carry would be delta-neutral
  but requires a two-leg implementation outside the frozen spec).
- The trailing-sign signal is a naive carry proxy; a more sophisticated carry
  definition would be a **new** hypothesis, not a tweak to this one (and is not
  authorised here).
- Test window is non-pristine.
- No directional stop (as specified): a risk-control gap, recorded.

## 8. Decision

**FAIL.** Carry as preregistered does not provide a robust standalone return on
this universe: it failed out-of-sample and it did not actually earn funding
(net funding PnL negative). No combination (P3-EXP-005) may include it.
Recorded in the registry `P3-EXP-003`.
