## 1. Objective

Does the genuine-futures Turtle **long** baseline survive realistic and stressed
fee/slippage scenarios? (Cost characterisation, not a rescue.)

## 2. Hypothesis / null

- H: a genuine edge degrades monotonically with cost but stays net-positive at
  plausible futures costs.
- Null: the edge is explained by costs.

## 3. Method — preregistered grid

fee per side ∈ {0.05%, 0.10%, 0.20%} × slippage per side ∈ {0.00%, 0.05%, 0.15%}.
freqtrade models slippage only through the fee parameter, so the **effective**
per-side cost is `fee + slippage`. Cells collapse to **six unique effective
costs**, run once each (mapping recorded in `P3-EXP-002.raw.json`):

| Effective cost/side | Grid cell(s) |
|--------------------:|--------------|
| 0.05% | 0.05% + 0.00% |
| 0.10% | 0.05% + 0.05% ; 0.10% + 0.00% |
| 0.15% | 0.10% + 0.05% |
| 0.20% | 0.05% + 0.15% ; 0.20% + 0.00% |
| 0.25% | 0.10% + 0.15% ; 0.20% + 0.05% |
| **0.35% (stress)** | **0.20% + 0.15%** |

Signal, stops, exits and sizing are **exactly** P3-EXP-001's (no change).

| Criterion | Threshold | long_vol | long_raw |

| PF at the stress scenario (full sample) | ≥ 1.05 | **1.051 ✓** | **1.340 ✓** |

| Cost pattern monotone / explainable | required | yes ✓ | yes ✓ |

**Verdict: PASS** on the preregistered cost-robustness criteria for the
full-sample long baseline.

**Scope note (essential):** this is a *cost* result only. The strategy already
failed P3-EXP-001 on out-of-sample expectancy and PF at base cost, and the
OOS test window degrades further as costs rise (PF 0.63–0.68 at stress). Passing
the cost criterion therefore **confers no validation** and does not change the
programme verdict.

## 6. Findings

1. **Monotone, expected degradation.** Full-sample PF falls smoothly as cost
   rises for both arms; no discontinuity or unexplained pattern.
2. **Costs are not the binding constraint.** Even at 7× the base cost the
   full-sample PF stays ≥ 1.05 (long_vol) and ≥ 1.34 (long_raw). This matches the
   earlier spot finding (EXP-002) that ordinary costs do not cause the failure.
3. **Sizing dominates cost sensitivity.** `long_raw` pays 10,417 USDT in fees at
   stress and keeps PF 1.340; `long_vol` pays 167,983 USDT and keeps PF 1.051 with
   an 81% drawdown. The volatility-scaled arm is far more cost- and
   drawdown-exposed for a lower profit factor — consistent with the P3-EXP-001
   ablation.
4. **OOS is the failure, not cost.** The 2025–2026 window is negative at every
   cost level and worsens with cost.

## 7. Limitations

- Test window is **non-pristine** (observed by earlier experiments).
- Slippage is a flat per-side adder, not a spread/impact model.
- Funding is not applied to positions here (deferred; funding data exists).
- No parameter sweep (by design).

## 8. Decision

**PASS (cost robustness, full sample)** — recorded exactly per the preregistered
criteria. The experiment confirms that costs are not the cause of the Turtle
failure. The long baseline remains **rejected** overall on OOS grounds
(P3-EXP-001), and no promotion or combination is authorised.

Recorded in the registry `P3-EXP-002`.
