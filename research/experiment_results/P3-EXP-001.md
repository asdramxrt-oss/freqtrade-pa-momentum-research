## 1. Objective

Does a plain Donchian/ATR Turtle system produce a robust edge on **genuine
futures OHLCV** after realistic costs — long and short separately — and how much
of any measured performance is the **signal** vs **volatility-scaled sizing**?

## 2. Hypothesis / null

- H: Turtle has a small positive OOS expectancy on genuine futures; a material
  part of measured performance comes from volatility-scaled sizing (Kim/Tse/Wald 2016).
- Null: OOS expectancy is indistinguishable from zero.

## 3. Method (exact)

Four arms (direction × sizing), all with the frozen signal (entry 20 / exit 10 /
ATR 20 / stop 2×ATR / ROI off / 1× leverage):

| Arm | Direction | Sizing |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| `long_vol` | long | 1% risk at the 2×ATR stop (volatility scaling) |
| `long_raw` | long | fixed notional (config stake; **no** volatility scaling) |
| `short_vol` | short | volatility scaling |
| `short_raw` | short | fixed notional |

```powershell
python -m freqtrade backtesting --userdir user_data -d user_data/data_p3 `
  --strategy-path research_lib/strategies -c user_data/configs/P3-EXP-001.json `
  --strategy <ARM> --timerange <WINDOW> --cache none --export trades --fee 0.0005
```

Windows: full `20190908-20260916`; train `→20221231`; validation `20230101-20241231`;
test `20250101-20260916` (**non-pristine**).

## 5. Key finding — signal vs sizing ablation

Same signal, different sizing, full sample:

| Sizing | Trades | PF | Wallet DD | p-value | Net (compounded) |
|--------|-------:|----:|----------:|--------:|-----------------:|
| fixed notional (raw) | 1482 | **1.602** | **12.5%** | **0.0005** | +252.9% |
| volatility-scaled | 1326 | 1.171 | 55.9% | 0.1380 | +2453.2% |

**Observed fact:** volatility-scaled sizing produced a ~10× larger *compounded*
headline return but a **lower profit factor** (1.17 vs 1.60), **4.5× the
drawdown** (55.9% vs 12.5%), and a **non-significant** expectancy (p=0.138 vs
p=0.0005). On this universe and signal the compounded headline is a
sizing/compounding artifact; the fixed-notional version is the more stable and
statistically supported configuration.

**Interpretation:** consistent with the literature critique — the apparent
trend-following performance is substantially a sizing effect, not extra signal
edge. The ablation was preregistered; nothing was tuned.

positive edge out-of-sample, for either direction. The short side is negative
throughout. The signal/sizing ablation shows the volatility-scaled compounded
headline overstates the edge while worsening drawdown and profit factor.

Consequences: P3-EXP-002 (cost/slippage) still applies to the *long* baseline as
a cost-robustness check, but no long Turtle configuration may be promoted. No
combination (P3-EXP-005) may proceed on this basis.
