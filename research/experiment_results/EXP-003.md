## 11. Limitations

1. **Futures-mode deviation.** Shorts cannot be expressed in spot, and Binance
   support is spot/futures only, so short/combined runs use futures mode at 1×
   leverage on a **mirror of the identical spot candles** (funding excluded). The
   spot long control is the check: 607 trades in both modes, PF 1.5666 (spot) vs
   1.5671 (futures), return +717.93% vs +718.62% — the mode is immaterial for
   longs. Wallet drawdown differs (28.64% spot vs 24.43% futures) because the
   futures wallet is tracked differently.
2. **One OOS evaluation per variant.** Preliminary, not confirmatory. The long
   pass would need a fresh holdout to be trusted further.
3. **The long-only result, while broad, is still a 1× spot-style result** with
   +718% driven by compounding; per-trade expectancy (118 USDT) and PF (1.567) are
   the comparable numbers.
4. **Geometry does not discriminate winners from losers**, so the apparent edge
   is not explained by any single structural variable measured here.
5. **Combined ≠ sum of sides.** Combining longs and shorts in one wallet is worse
   than either side alone in-sample because they compete for the same five slots;
   this is a portfolio effect, not a signal effect, and is not optimised here.
6. **Pivot look-ahead** is the highest-risk failure mode for a structure strategy;
   it is mitigated by the publication delay in `pa_structure` and covered by
   prefix-invariance tests, but the tests exercise synthetic and historical data,
   not a live feed.
7. **Effective sample size** remains below the trade count (co-moving crypto pairs).

## 12. Decision

**MIXED / REQUIRES FURTHER VALIDATION.**

- The pre-registered **combined** object **FAILS** the acceptance criteria
  (4/8: A3, A4, A5, A6), so pullback continuation is **not** validated as a
  combined strategy.
- The failure is caused entirely by the **short side**, which is decisively
  negative (PF 0.73, −55.95%, 61.73% drawdown, expectancy significantly
  *negative* at p = 0.0049, only 1/7 profitable years, 9/10 pairs negative). The
  short side is **rejected**.
- The **long side independently passes all eight criteria** (PF 1.567, sub-25%
  drawdown, expectancy significant at p = 0.011, positive OOS +14.01%, 6/7
  profitable years, all 10 pairs profitable). This is the first result in the
  programme to clear the full checklist, and it is a real finding — but it is a
  pre-registered *variant*, not the primary object, and it rests on a single
  holdout evaluation.
- The long side is therefore **promising but not validated**; it warrants a
  dedicated, explicitly-authorised follow-up experiment with its own
  pre-registered OOS protocol. It is **not** production-ready and is **not**
  called robust.
- No parameter was tuned, no criterion was changed, no side was disabled, and no
  geometry filter was fitted after the fact.

Consequences for the programme:

1. The combined pullback strategy is not promoted and not combined with anything.
2. The short-continuation geometry is recorded as rejected for this universe.
3. EXP-004 (Turtle + Pullback) is **not** started automatically. The next
   experiment requires an explicit decision.
4. The long-only pullback hypothesis is the strongest lead so far and should be
   the explicit subject of the next authorisation decision.
